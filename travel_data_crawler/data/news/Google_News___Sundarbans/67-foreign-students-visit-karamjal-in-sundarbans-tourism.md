67 foreign students visit Karamjal in Sundarbans
Photo : BSS
BAGERHAT, May 28, 2025 (BSS) - A total of 67 trainees from eight different countries, currently enrolled at the Defence Services Command and Staff College, visited the Karamjal Wildlife Breeding Centre in Mongla upazila of the district.
Acting officer-in-charge of the Karamjal Wildlife Breeding Center, Hawlader Azad Kabir, on Tuesday welcomed the 67 trainees and seven officials from countries including Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, and India.
The visit was an integral part of the two-year training course. During the visit, they also explored other significant areas of the Sundarbans.
