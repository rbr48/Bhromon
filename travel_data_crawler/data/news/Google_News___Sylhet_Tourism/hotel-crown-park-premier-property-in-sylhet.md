Hotel Crown Park, premier property in Sylhet
A Monitor Report
Published: September 01, 2024 | 12:00 AM
2 min read
Print
Sylhet : Mark your calendars for September 2024 as Crown Park Ltd, Sylhet's newest four-star property, makes its grand debut.
Strategically positioned for ultimate convenience, the hotel provides guests with effortless access to Sylhet's airport, bus stand, train station and major tourist and corporate hubs.
With 43 luxurious rooms, two elegant banquet halls, a state-of-the-art gym and a welcoming café, Crown Park Ltd is tailored to meet the needs of both tourists and families, said a release.
Their diverse dining options include a multi-cuisine restaurant and a rooftop Arabian eatery, complemented by a stunning swimming pool, ensuring every guest experiences a blend of comfort and luxury, added the release.
The hotel has announced the appointment of Faisal Abdullah as its General Manager.
Renowned for his client relations and extensive expertise, Abdullah's leadership is set to elevate Crown Park Ltd to new heights of excellence, claimed the hotel in the release.
His commitment to providing quality service ensures that every guest will enjoy a memorable stay, concluded the hotel-"where luxury meets convenience amidst the region's natural beauty".
