Biman's round-trip tickets from Dhaka to Ctg, Sylhet at BDT 3,000
A Monitor Report
Published: April 16, 2023 | 12:00 AM
1 min read
Print
Dhaka : Biman Bangladesh Airlines is offering round-trip tickets for Dhaka- Chattogram-Dhaka and Dhaka-Sylhet-Dhaka flights at BDT 3,000.
The offer is available from April 15 to April 30 on the occasion of Eid al-Fitr, said Biman Managing Director and CEO Shafiul Azim, as per reports.
"Besides, passengers can avail a 5 per cent discount on all domestic and international route tickets using Biman's promo code," he added.
Also, the airline will operate additional flights to meet extra demand on the domestic market.
The additional frequencies are as follow: the national flag carrier Biman Bangladesh Airlines will operate two flights on April 18, 29, three flights on April 25 and five flights on April 19, 20, 21 and 24.
On April 22, Biman will only operate one flight.
