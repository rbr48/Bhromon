Tourism & Hospitality Management Department Organizes Study Tour to Bangladesh Travel and Tourism Fair
Events Date:
October
30
Year:
2025
Organized By:
Department of Tourism and Hospitality Management
Venue:
Bangladesh-China Friendship Conference Centre (BCFCC)
The Department of Tourism & Hospitality Management (THM) under the Faculty of Business Administration (FBA) at the American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB) organized a study tour to the 13
th
Bangladesh Travel and Tourism Fair at Bangladesh-China Friendship Conference Centre on October 30, 2025.
The BTTF Fair was organized by the
Tour Operators Association of Bangladesh (TOAB)
with
Biman Bangladesh Airlines
as the title sponsor, the three-day event featured participants from
12 countries
, including Pakistan, Nepal, Bhutan, the Maldives, Singapore, Vietnam, China, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Malaysia, and Turkey. With
over 220 stalls
across
20 pavilions
,
the fair highlighted airlines, hotels, resorts, tour operators, and healthcare tourism services. The students attended the inauguration ceremony of the program where a good number of distinguished guests delivered their valuable speeches. The students visited the stalls and were delighted to be present at the event.
The study tour provided valuable insights for students of Tourism and Hospitality Management, aligning with the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) of "Quality Education."  and
“
Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
.”
This initiative reflects AIUB's commitment to educational excellence and its dedication to nurturing future professionals in the domain of tourism and hospitality management.
The event was coordinated by Ms. Sharmin Sultana, Assistant Professor, Department of Tourism and Hospitality Management under the close supervision of Prof. Dr. Partha Prasad Chowdhury, Chartered Marketer (UK), Head, Department of Marketing and THM. The Department of Tourism and Hospitality Management (THM), FBA expresses sincere appreciation for all administrative and logistical support received from AIUB Management.
