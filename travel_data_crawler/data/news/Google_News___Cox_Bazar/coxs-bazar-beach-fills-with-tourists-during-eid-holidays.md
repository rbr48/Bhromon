# Cox’s Bazar beach fills with tourists during Eid holidays

Country’s top tourist destination Cox’s Bazar began witnessing a surge of visitors during the Eid-ul-Azha holidays as thousands flocked to the world’s longest sea beach.
On the second day of Eid on Friday, large crowds of tourists were seen at Laboni, Sugandha, Kalatali and Daryanagar points from the morning.
Many visitors enjoyed sea bathing, family outings and photography along the beach, while others gathered to relax after the busy Eid celebrations.
Tourists from different parts of the country said they came to Cox’s Bazar to spend quality time with their families and enjoy the festive atmosphere.
“I came to Cox’s Bazar with my family. My children are very excited to see the sea. Spending Eid holidays here is truly enjoyable,” said Sohel Rana, a tourist from Feni.
Nilima, a visitor from Kushtia, said the beach environment, sea waves and festive mood made their trip memorable.
Tourism insiders said around 30 percent of hotel rooms in Cox’s Bazar had been booked by the second day of Eid, with nearly 40,000 tourists already gathering in the coastal town.
Cox’s Bazar Hotel Owners’ Association General Secretary Mukim Khan said tourist numbers are usually lower during the first two days of Eid due to sacrificial activities, but visitor arrivals have already started increasing.
He expressed hope that the number of tourists would rise further over the next few days.
To ensure safety, lifeguards have been deployed at different points along the beach, while red flags were placed in risky areas to warn visitors.
“We are on maximum alert for tourist safety. Everyone is being advised to remain cautious while entering the sea,” said lifeguard Joynal Abedin.
Law enforcement agencies have also strengthened security across the tourist zone.
Additional Superintendent of Police Ohidur Rahman said tourist police, district police and plainclothes officers were deployed at key locations to maintain security during the holidays.
Hoteliers said tourist pressure may increase further during the remaining Eid vacation and advised visitors to book accommodations in advance to avoid inconvenience.
With roaring waves, blue waters and a festive mood, Cox’s Bazar has once again turned vibrant during the Eid holidays.
