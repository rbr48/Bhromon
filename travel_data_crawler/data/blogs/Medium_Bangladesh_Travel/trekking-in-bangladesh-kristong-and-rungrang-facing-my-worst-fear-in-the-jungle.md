So, I’m arachnophobic. Like,
really
arachnophobic.
I don’t remember when this fear started, but I’ve literally refused to use the bathroom if there was a spider in it. I’ve waited almost entire days for my father to come home just so he could remove a spider and I could finally pee. It’s not rational. I know that. But I can’t control it.
This is important context for what happened next.
In 2021, I’d just come back to Bangladesh after finishing my master’s in Norway. I was still in my adventure phase — I’d done Trolltunga, climbed those 1,400-something stairs at Reinebringen in the Lofoten Islands, and was feeling pretty confident about my trekking abilities. If you’ve read my Trolltunga story
Part 1
and
Part 2
, you know the version of me I’m talking about — confident, stubborn, and slightly delusional about my stamina.
So when my friend Prova apu (apu means big sister in Bengali) invited me to join her on a four-day trek to
Kristong and Rungrang
— one of the highest peaks in the Bandarban region of the Chittagong Hill Tracts— I just said yes. Didn’t research. Didn’t think twice. Just bought a bus ticket to Cox’s Bazar, where apu lived, and showed up.
