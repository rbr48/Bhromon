For over thirty years I wanted to go to Bhutan. It was only a dream, because I’m woefully cheap and constantly doing the following math:
X dollars/day here equals X dollars/2 days over there, or maybe 3 days, or more
Hence, I go over there. I am so often just looking for ways to extend the trip with a fixed budget.
So Bhutan stayed well out of reach. I came geographically close to it a few times in the past, but never seriously looked into it. For those who don’t know the particularities, travel to Bhutan (for citizens of all countries except India, Bangladesh, and the Maldives) involves the following:
A purchased tour package with a set time limit, from a government-authorized tour company. Most excursions I’ve seen run from four days up to three weeks.
With the above, a tour guide who will be fully in charge of everywhere you go, with previously arranged accommodation and transportation. Tourists are not allowed to venture off independently.
