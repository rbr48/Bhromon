# Bhromon TVC Script: "স্মৃতির মানচিত্র" (Map of Memories)

**Target Audience**: Youth, backpackers, adventure seekers, and casual travelers in Bangladesh.  
**Tone**: Atmospheric, inspiring, warm, and tech-forward.  
**Duration**: 60 Seconds  
**Languages**: Bengali (Dialogue/Voiceover) & English (UI/Subtitles)

## 💡 Director's Notes & Visual Style

### 🎨 Color Palette
- The visual styling mimics the Bhromon app: sleek dark modes mixed with vibrant, organic local colors ( Bandarban green, Sundarban clay, Sajek gold).
- **Polaroid Style Transitions**: Major transitions between scenes utilize the polaroid-style share cards generated by the app.

### 🎤 Cast & Acting
- Natural, unpretentious acting. Real travelers with backpacks, mud on their boots, and genuine smiles. 

### 📱 UI Representation
- Screen overlays are clean and crisp, highlighting key elements: **District Map Widget**, **Voice Note Waveforms**, and **Trust Badges** without cluttering the screen.
