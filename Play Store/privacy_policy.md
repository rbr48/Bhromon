# Privacy Policy for Bhromon

**Last Updated: June 23, 2026**

Welcome to **Bhromon** (the "App"), a travel companion and log journaling application. Bhromon is owned and operated by the Izhaan Intellect ("we," "our," or "us"). 

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data
To provide our core mapping and travel features (such as locating nearby travel destination pins, showing your location on the map, and calculating movement speeds for adaptive tracking), the App requires access to your device's location services.
* **Precise Location (GPS)**: We collect precise location data when the App is in use.
* **Background Location**: If enabled by the user, the App may access your location in the background to calculate speed profiles for tracking transitions.
* **User Control**: You can enable or disable location access at any time through your device settings. If disabled, location-based map features will not function.

### B. Offline Database & Storage
* **Cached Content**: Travel logs, drafts, point-of-interest (POI) caches, and authentication profiles are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database (powered by Drift).
* **Storage Access**: The App accesses local storage to save offline draft logs, images, and downloaded map tiles (offline maps feature).

### C. Personal & Authentication Data
* **User Profile**: We collect your email address, display name, and login credentials when you create an account.
* **Authentication**: All user login sessions are authenticated securely.

### D. Device & Technical Information
* **Device Integrity**: The App performs security and integrity checks (such as detecting jailbroken or rooted devices) to protect communication safety and vault encryption.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* **To Provide and Maintain the Service**: Display travel logs, maps, offline routing, and weather information.
* **Proximity Features**: Identify tourist pins in your vicinity.
* **Offline Syncing**: Securely sync local draft changes back to Firestore once an internet connection is established.
* **Security & Prevention**: Maintain device integrity checks and prevent fraudulent activity.
* **Push Notifications**: Trigger in-app alerts and notifications on chats or new updates.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following scenarios:
* **Firebase Services**: User profiles, chat messages, and public travel logs are stored in Google Firebase / Firestore. This data is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
* **Legal Compliance**: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

---

## 4. Data Security
The security of your data is important to us.
* We implement SSL Pinning across all network requests to ensure communication with our servers is secure and cannot be intercepted.
* Offline data is stored using native encryption options on the device.
* However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights & Deletion
You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information.
* **Account Deletion**: You can request the deletion of your account and associated travel logs at any time from within the App settings.
* **Data Portability**: You may request a copy of your stored logs.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
