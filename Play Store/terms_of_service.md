## 2. Core Subsystems & Scope of Use

1. **Universal Tour Engine & Roadmaps**: Supports custom itineraries, multi-stop tours, highway checkpoints, and route distance estimations.
2. **Multi-Family Group Ledger & Settle-Up**: Provides integer-paisa expense accounting, flexible splitting (equal, family-weighted, exact, or share-based), and payment settlement matrices.
3. **Real-Time Voice Lounge & Calling**: Discord-style group voice lounges and 1-on-1 audio/video calls using WebRTC mesh and Coturn STUN/TURN servers.
4. **Family Safety Hub & SOS Siren**: Emergency alerting system that broadcasts alarms and transient location coordinates to fellow tour members.
5. **Smart Newspaper Hub**: Built-in ad-free news reader with client-side network blocking and cosmetic filtering for clean reading.
6. **Family Game Zone**: Pure-Dart deterministic board and card games (Card 29, Chess AI, Ludo Express, Teen Patti, Carrom).

## 4. Tour Group Governance & Role Hierarchy

Bhromon operates on a membership-based group access model:
* **Roles**: Tour groups feature three distinct roles: `owner`, `admin`, and `member`.
* **Owner Authority**: Only the tour owner can transfer ownership, alter root invite codes, or promote members to admin status.
* **Admin Authority**: Admins can edit itineraries, configure vehicle layouts, manage room maps, pin or delete notices, and moderate channel posts.
* **Membership Bans & Removals**: Admins and owners reserve the right to remove or ban disruptive members from their tour group.
* **Invite Codes**: Joining a group requires an explicit 8-character invite code shared by the tour organizer. Banned accounts cannot rejoin.
