2. Platform Overview

Bhromon provides an all-in-one suite of non-commercial digital travel tools designed to organize journeys, connect friends and families, and safeguard travelers:

  • Universal Tour Engine: Multi-stop itinerary planning across 40+ presets in Bangladesh, route roadmaps, detour factor estimations, and checkpoint progress tracking.
  • Group Ledger & Settle-Up: Multi-family expense allocation in integer paisa precision, budget tracking, and cash settlement calculation.
  • Calling & Voice Lounges: Real-time WebRTC mesh group voice lounges and 1-on-1 audio/video calls.
  • Family Safety Hub: Live SOS emergency sirens, location dispatch to tour members, and verified emergency contact directories.
  • Smart Newspaper Hub: Distraction-free reader mode with client-side ad blocking and local text-to-speech audio narration.
  • Family Game Zone: Pure-Dart deterministic board and card games (Card 29, Chess AI, Ludo Express, Teen Patti, Carrom).

3. Free & Open Source Philosophy

Bhromon is built for the public benefit and operates strictly as free and open-source software (FOSS):

  • Zero Fees: There are no subscription fees, premium paywalls, paid feature gates, or in-app microtransactions.
  • No Commercialization: We do not sell user data to advertising networks or third-party behavioral trackers.
  • Community Stewardship: Source code is published transparently on GitHub (github.com/rbr48/Bhromon) for collaborative development and auditing.

4. Tour Governance & Roles

Access to individual tour spaces is strictly membership-based:

  • Role Hierarchy: Each tour group has an owner, one or more admin members, and regular member accounts.
  • Owner Rights: The group owner retains ultimate governance over the tour, including altering invite codes and transferring ownership.
  • Admin Moderation: Admins have permission to manage itinerary stops, configure bus and hotel layouts, delete or pin messages, and remove disruptive participants.
  • Invite Codes: Entering a tour requires a valid 8-character invite code. Tour organizers have full discretion over whom they invite into their private trip.

6. Safety & Emergency SOS Disclaimer

Not a Replacement for Official Emergency Services

The Family Safety Hub and SOS siren feature are designed strictly for peer-to-peer alerting among your tour group members. In any life-threatening situation, accident, or crime, you must immediately contact official emergency services (such as Bangladesh Police / Fire Service via 999).

Navigation & Road Accuracy: Distance estimations, detour factors, route progress, and tide times are computer estimates. Actual conditions may change due to landslides, road construction, weather disruptions, or ferry queues. Always follow local law enforcement guidelines and practice defensive driving.

When participating in voice lounges, video calls, or sharing reels and photos, you agree to respect your fellow travelers:

  • Prohibited Content: You may not upload or transmit hate speech, nudity, violent content, harassment, copyright-infringing media, or malicious scripts.
