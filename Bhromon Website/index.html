FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bhromon completely free and ad-free?

Yes, 100%. Bhromon is free and open-source software with no business model, no paywalls, no subscriptions, and zero advertisements. All features — including WebRTC voice lounges, paisa-accurate ledgers, and game rooms — are completely free for everyone.

What types of groups are supported?

Bhromon supports 7 distinct group presets: Families, Offices & Teams, Campuses & Student Batches, Messes & Shared Flats, Housing Societies, Rotating Savings Groups (Samities), and Travel/Tour Groups. Each preset features dedicated tools such as daily meal counters, maintenance service requests, or samity payout rotation schedules.

How does the Paisa-Accurate Group Ledger work?

The ledger calculates all expenses down to exact integer paisa to prevent rounding discrepancies. It supports splitting by family count weights, equal shares, exact individual amounts, or custom shares. It also generates optimal settle-up transaction suggestions and allows one-click UTF-8 CSV exports.

How does the Family Safety Hub & Instant SOS alert work?

When triggered, the emergency siren sounds on your phone and instantly broadcasts your live GPS coordinates directly to all active members of your group via push notifications, ensuring immediate emergency visibility.
