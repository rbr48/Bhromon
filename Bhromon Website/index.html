FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does offline work?

The app keeps your group's notices, messages and records on your phone and syncs them when you're back online. Anything you add offline is sent once the connection returns.

Are shared expenses split fairly?

Each expense is split by the rule you choose: by family size, equally, by exact amounts or by shares. Amounts are kept in whole paisa, so the totals always add up.

How do trust tiers work?

Every member has a trust tier, from New Explorer to Pioneer. You can see your tier on the trust dashboard in the app.
