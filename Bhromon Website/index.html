FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Offline Vault work?

You can download entire district vector maps, guides, and contact directories while connected to Wi-Fi. The maps use local storage and offline GPS to track your position even without internet coverage.

Can I really trust the Total Budget estimates?

Yes! Budgets are verified through community Helpful Votes. Only highly rated logs by experienced users (Pioneer and above) are featured on top, and older budgets decay automatically as prices change.

How do I earn XP and level up my Explorer Tier?

By sharing accurate TripLogs with drawn routes, reporting incorrect pricing, verifying offline safety contacts, and receiving Helpful Votes from other travelers, you earn XP to advance through the tiers.
