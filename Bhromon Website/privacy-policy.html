Privacy Policy

Discover how Bhromon protects your privacy, secures your travel journals, and upholds zero-advertiser, zero-surveillance standards.

Version 3.0.0 | Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bhromon (the "Platform" or "App"). Bhromon is an all-in-one universal tour companion, trip ledger, and community travel hub owned and operated by Bhromon Technologies Ltd. ("we," "our," or "us").

We believe travel tools should respect human autonomy and confidentiality. Bhromon is completely free and open source: we do not sell your data, we do not partner with data brokers, and we never embed behavioral ad-trackers in our applications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information across our native Android application, Progressive Web Application (https://app.bhromon.fun), and website (https://bhromon.fun).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data to provide our travel planning, ledger accounting, and safety tools:

A. Account & Authentication Data

  • Account Credentials: When you sign up or log in via Firebase Auth (Email/Password or Google Sign-In), we store your email address, display name, and avatar URL.
  • Tour Memberships: Your membership roles (owner, admin, or member) and family slot settings are saved to organize vehicle seats, room allotments, and expense splits.

B. Trip Ledger & Organization Data

  • Ledger & Expenses: Shared costs, split modes (family-weighted, equal, exact paisa, or shares), and settlement records entered by you or your group organizers.
  • Logistics Maps: Bus seat selections, cabin berths, and hotel room assignments chosen for your tour.
Feature / Permission Purpose Storage / Processing
GPS Location Waypoint progress, ETA estimations, and Emergency SOS siren dispatch. Processed in foreground; transient broadcast only when SOS siren is activated.
Photo Picker Sharing trip memories, reels, and chat attachments. Zero broad storage permissions. Uses native system photo picker contract.

3. Location Data & Safety SOS

Because Bhromon is built for travel across Bangladesh's diverse highways, hill tracts, and river ways, location services are used carefully and transparently:

Foreground Navigation & Checkpoint Verification

While the app is open, your location coordinates may be used to verify arrival at road checkpoints (ferries, food stops, army checkposts) and calculate live distance to your next tour destination.

Family Safety Hub & Live SOS Siren

When an emergency SOS alert is sounded from the Family Safety Hub, your name and latest available GPS coordinates are dispatched to your tour group's active members and broadcast via high-priority Firebase push notifications. SOS alerts automatically resolve and expire after 6 hours.

Opt-In Control: Location access is entirely voluntary. You can revoke location permissions at any time in device settings.

4. Photos, Media & Dedicated VPS Storage

We respect Google Play's modern photo and video permissions policy (Target SDK 36):

  • No Broad Storage Permissions: We do not ask for or require READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO. Media selection is handled entirely through the privacy-preserving native Android Photo Picker.
  • Dedicated Self-Hosted VPS Media Server: To protect media from commercial third-party scanning, uploaded trip photos and vertical video reels are stored on our dedicated VPS container (https://bhromon.fun/api/media/) located in a high-security data center.
  • Token-Gated Authentication: Every media upload, stream, and deletion request is authenticated against your active Firebase ID token, guaranteeing only authorized tour members can access private trip reels.
  • Automated Orphan Purge: Uploaded media drafts that are discarded or unlinked after 30 minutes are automatically deleted from server disks to prevent digital clutter.

5. Real-Time Voice & Video Calling

Bhromon provides Discord-style drop-in voice lounges and 1-on-1 audio/video calls for tour groups:

  • End-to-End Encryption (DTLS-SRTP): Real-time voice and video streams are transmitted directly peer-to-peer or relayed through our Coturn STUN/TURN server using industry-standard DTLS-SRTP encryption.
  • Zero Voice Recording: We do not record, analyze, tap, or store audio or video streams. Once a call or voice lounge session ends, the audio stream ceases completely.
  • Hardware Permissions: Microphone and camera hardware are accessed strictly with your explicit prompt and only while an active call or voice session is connected.

6. News Hub & Zero-Tracking Browser

Our in-app Smart Newspaper reader provides clean, distraction-free reading of leading Bangladesh newspapers:

  • Client-Side AdBlock: The browser blocks invasive tracking scripts, popups, and advertisement domains directly on the device using comprehensive EasyList filters.
  • Zero Browsing History Collection: We never log, profile, sell, or transmit the articles you read, topics you search, or websites you visit.
  • Offline Text-to-Speech (TTS): Bengali and English speech synthesis for article listening executes on-device without sending article text to external speech harvesting APIs.

7. How We Use Information

We use information exclusively to maintain and power our application services:

  • Tour Coordination: Calculating itinerary milestones, dynamic routes, and highway checkpoints.
  • Group Ledger Accounting: Computing multi-family expenses down to integer paisa, maintaining budget balances, and generating settlement matrices.
  • Offline Relational Caches: Caching checklists, tour members, and seat mappings in Drift SQLite and encrypted Hive storage for offline hill tracts access.
  • Safety Notifications: Dispatching urgent SOS alerts and channel mentions through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

8. Dedicated VPS & Cloud Infrastructure

Our infrastructure is architected for maximum user sovereignty and performance:

  • Hostinger VPS (Self-Hosted Media & API): Hosts our Dockerized media microservice and Coturn WebRTC signaling on an isolated Linux VPS (148.135.137.245), protected by Cloudflare SSL.
  • Google Firebase & Firestore: Manages user authentication and real-time database synchronization under strict Firestore Security Rules.
  • OpenStreetMap (OSM): Supplies public geographic map tiles. No personal identity is ever shared with OSM.

9. Data Security Measures

Security is baked into the foundation of Bhromon:

  • Transport Layer Security: All web and API traffic is forced over HTTPS with modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Database Access Control: Firestore security rules enforce that tour feeds, chats, and ledger expenses can be read or written exclusively by active members of that specific tour group.
  • Local Storage Sandboxing: On-device SQLite databases and Hive preference boxes are stored in the application's secure private sandbox.

10. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

You retain full sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • Permanent Account Deletion: You can delete your account and personal profile permanently at any time through the in-app settings or by contacting our team.
  • Media Deletion: Deleting a photo or video from a tour post or reel immediately purges the corresponding file from our VPS media storage.
  • Ledger Export (Data Portability): Tour admins and members can export the entire tour ledger to UTF-8 CSV at any point.
